We introduce TraceCompass, a lightweight attribution layer that highlights which retrieved spans support each answer sentence in retrieval-augmented generation. The method combines attention re-weighting with a contrastive span scorer and requires no retraining of the base model. Experiments on three open-domain QA benchmarks show consistent improvements in faithfulness while preserving answer quality.
